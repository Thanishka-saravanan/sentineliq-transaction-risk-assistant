# SentinelIQ Bank Fraud Desk — Transaction Risk Policy

**Document Reference**: POL-FRD-2024-PS06  
**Track**: Banking — TRACK_ID=PS06  
**Effective Date**: January 2024  
**Applicability**: Tier-1 Retail & Commercial Account Risk Monitoring  

---

## Important Regulatory & Operating Notice

> **IMPORTANT**: The system identifies activity requiring human review. A risk finding does not establish that fraud has occurred.
>
> All flags, deviations, and statistical anomalies generated by this automated framework represent prioritized indicators for human fraud analysts and compliance investigators. The system provides auditable evidence trails and behavioural comparisons to accelerate investigation, but the ultimate determination of legitimacy, fraud, or false positive rests solely with certified human investigators.

---

## Risk Policy Framework

SentinelIQ operates under a dual-layer risk architecture:
1. **Deterministic Baseline & Anomaly Engine**: Establishes individualized behavioural profiles per account and calculates exact mathematical deviations against defined thresholds.
2. **Grounded Investigator Reasoning**: Translates numerical evidence into contextual, auditable findings grounded in this policy document without hallucination or premature fraud accusation.

Below are the operational risk rules governing all retail transaction investigations.

---

### Rule R01 — Unusually Large Transfer

- **Rule ID**: `R01`
- **Title**: Unusually Large Transfer
- **Purpose**: Identify outward fund movements that are materially and statistically disparate from the account holder's established historical spending distribution.
- **Detection Criteria**:
  - Transaction amount exceeds `5.0x` the customer's historical transaction median, OR
  - Transaction amount exceeds `4.0x` the customer's historical mean plus two standard deviations, AND
  - Transaction amount exceeds the customer's historical maximum transaction by at least `150%`.
  - Absolute floor check: Transactions under \$1,000.00 are exempted from R01 to prevent false alarms on low-balance micro-accounts.
- **Evidence Required**:
  - Exact `transaction_id`, timestamp, and transfer amount.
  - Customer baseline metrics: historical median amount, historical mean amount, historical maximum amount.
  - Absolute and percentage deviation calculated against the customer's median and historical max.
  - Transaction channel (e.g., NEFT, IMPS, NET_BANKING, WIRE).
- **Severity Guidance**:
  - **High**: Deviation > 15x historical median, or transfer amount > \$15,000.00.
  - **Medium**: Deviation between 5x and 15x historical median.
  - **Low**: Deviation near threshold with plausible narrative context.
- **Investigator Action Guidance**:
  1. Inspect customer KYC profile, registered employment/income tier, and source of funds.
  2. Verify beneficiary details (`payee`) against external sanctions and watchlists.
  3. Contact the customer via out-of-band verified phone channel before releasing outbound settlement holds if amount is critical.
  4. Review account balance history to determine if incoming credits preceded the large debit (pass-through account indicator).
- **Limitations**:
  - May trigger on legitimate life events (e.g., property down-payment, vehicle purchase, hospital emergency, annual tuition payment).
  - Does not evaluate beneficiary reputational risk without external intelligence feeds.

### Rule R02 — New Payee Payment Burst

- **Rule ID**: `R02`
- **Title**: New Payee Payment Burst
- **Purpose**: Detect potential account takeover (ATO) or social engineering scams characterized by high-velocity consecutive payments to an unfamiliar beneficiary newly introduced to the account.
- **Detection Criteria**:
  - Payee has no prior transaction history in the customer's historical baseline (`first_seen = True`).
  - At least 3 transactions occur to the same new payee within a rolling 2-hour window, OR 2 or more transactions totaling > \$5,000.00 within a 1-hour window.
  - Transactions use instant settlement channels (e.g., IMPS, UPI, Real-Time Rails).
- **Evidence Required**:
  - Exact `transaction_ids` in the burst cluster.
  - Payee name and confirmation that payee was absent from prior account history.
  - Number of burst transactions, individual amounts, and aggregate burst sum.
  - Time delta between the first and last transaction in the cluster (e.g., 42 minutes).
- **Severity Guidance**:
  - **High**: Aggregate burst amount > \$5,000.00 across 3+ transfers within 90 minutes.
  - **Medium**: 2–3 transactions totaling between \$2,000.00 and \$5,000.00 within 2 hours.
  - **Low**: Micro-burst totaling < \$2,000.00 where payee registration was authenticated with multi-factor authentication.
- **Investigator Action Guidance**:
  1. Review payee addition timestamp and channel (e.g., mobile app vs web portal).
  2. Check if authentication credentials or SIM/device bindings were modified prior to the burst.
  3. Determine whether beneficiary account is held at a high-risk institution or digital wallet.
  4. Initiate temporary hold on pending outbound rails if burst is ongoing.
- **Limitations**:
  - Legitimate contractor settlements, home renovation milestones, or emergency peer-to-peer transfers may exhibit burst characteristics.
  - Does not account for pre-scheduled recurring payroll or corporate bulk disbursements.

### Rule R03 — Odd-Hours Activity

- **Rule ID**: `R03`
- **Title**: Odd-Hours Activity
- **Purpose**: Flag transactions executed during high-risk nocturnal timeframes that fundamentally contradict the account owner's proven behavioural schedule.
- **Detection Criteria**:
  - Transaction timestamp falls within the defined high-risk window: `00:00:00` to `05:00:00` local time.
  - Customer's historical active profile shows < 5% of lifetime transactions occurring during this window (strictly daytime/evening user).
  - Transaction is an outward debit or transfer exceeding \$500.00.
- **Evidence Required**:
  - Exact `transaction_id`, transaction time, and amount.
  - Customer's historical operating window (e.g., 96% of activity between 08:30 and 20:30).
  - Deviation explanation indicating total absence of nocturnal transfers in prior months.
  - Channel and device login metadata if available.
- **Severity Guidance**:
  - **High**: High-value transfer (> \$3,000.00) between 01:00 AM and 04:30 AM with zero historical precedent.
  - **Medium**: Moderate value transfer (\$500.00–\$3,000.00) during the window.
  - **Low**: Routine subscription charge, automated utility direct debit, or small travel expenditure.
- **Investigator Action Guidance**:
  1. Differentiate between merchant-initiated batch debits (e.g., Netflix subscription recurring at 02:00 AM) and customer-initiated transfers (e.g., manual NET_BANKING transfer).
  2. Inspect IP geolocation and device fingerprint for nocturnal sessions.
  3. Correlate with concurrent SMS delivery or OTP generation alerts.
- **Limitations**:
  - Shift workers, international travelers, and nocturnal lifestyle customers will have non-standard baselines. The engine must adjust to customer-specific distributions rather than rigid global curfew rules.
  - Automated merchant billings often post during overnight batch processing.

### Rule R04 — Customer Behaviour Deviation

- **Rule ID**: `R04`
- **Title**: Customer Behaviour Deviation
- **Purpose**: Capture compound anomalies where no single threshold alone breaches critical limits, but multiple dimensions (amount, channel, payee, and timing) simultaneously diverge from personal norms.
- **Detection Criteria**:
  - Multi-factor score computed across:
    - Amount divergence (e.g., 3x–5x personal median).
    - Unfamiliar payee category.
    - Channel divergence (e.g., frequent UPI user suddenly executing high-value NET_BANKING wire).
    - Elevated velocity (e.g., daily transaction count doubling weekly average).
  - Composite anomaly index exceeds risk tolerance threshold.
- **Evidence Required**:
  - Specific `transaction_id`(s) triggering the compound divergence.
  - Baseline metrics for channel usage frequency, typical amount percentiles, and typical payee categories.
  - Explanation of the multiple dimensions of divergence.
- **Severity Guidance**:
  - **Medium**: Concurrent divergence across 2–3 behavioral dimensions without catastrophic financial exposure.
  - **Low**: Borderline divergence in one dimension with slight elevation in frequency.
- **Investigator Action Guidance**:
  1. Review recent customer communication logs or inquiries (e.g., travel notifications, limit enhancement requests).
  2. Examine preceding and subsequent transaction context to assess organic vs inorganic spend patterns.
  3. Validate if the customer recently updated mobile apps or banking platform credentials.
- **Limitations**:
  - Holiday seasons, weddings, relocations, and major life changes naturally alter spending dimensions.
  - Requires sufficient transaction depth (minimum 15–20 transactions) to establish an authentic baseline.

### Rule R05 — Linked Transaction Pattern

- **Rule ID**: `R05`
- **Title**: Linked Transaction Pattern
- **Purpose**: Connect separate transactions that form part of a unified, coordinated sequence or attack vector, such as probing micro-debits followed by rapid high-value account liquidation.
- **Detection Criteria**:
  - Sequence linkage: A nominal verification transfer (e.g., \$1.00, \$5.00) followed within 15–60 minutes by rapid high-value transfers to the identical payee.
  - Smurfing / structuring linkage: Multiple successive transfers just below regulatory reporting thresholds (e.g., \$4,800–\$4,990) within a condensed timeframe.
  - Cross-rule correlation: Occurrence of `R02` (New Payee Burst) + `R03` (Odd Hours) + `R01` (Large Amount) within the same session.
- **Evidence Required**:
  - Ordered chronological list of all linked `transaction_ids`.
  - Narrative timeline highlighting progression from trigger (e.g., probe) to climax (drain).
  - Total cumulative monetary exposure across the chain.
  - Identification of common threads (same beneficiary, same channel, rapid sequence).
- **Severity Guidance**:
  - **Critical**: Probing transaction followed by sequential high-value drain during off-hours.
  - **High**: Multiple structured transfers approaching daily velocity limits to an unverified beneficiary.
  - **Medium**: Clustered transfers with partial linkage and ambiguous commercial purpose.
- **Investigator Action Guidance**:
  1. Immediately quarantine remaining account liquid balance to prevent further automated drain.
  2. Trace beneficiary account IFSC/routing number and initiate inter-bank recall protocols.
  3. Contact customer's verified emergency contact or secondary verified phone number.
  4. Flag session IP address, IMEI/device identifiers across bank fraud consortia.
- **Limitations**:
  - Legitimate business testing or split invoice payments may mimic linked transfer sequences.
  - Requires holistic timeline reconstruction across multiple transaction records.

## Conclusion & Governance

All risk rules are subject to quarterly model risk management (MRM) review, false-positive tuning, and regulatory audit. Findings produced using this policy must maintain transparent mathematical traceability to underlying transaction IDs and baseline statistics.
